Md Shahidul Khan Pappo

AI/ML Researcher · Trustworthy AI for Healthcare · Medical Imaging · LLM Agents

I am a computational AI researcher building trustworthy, explainable, and reliable AI for healthcare. I work on medical image analysis, calibrated federated learning under distribution shift, post-hoc interpretability (XAI), and LLM/agent systems that help clinicians understand why a model makes a prediction — not only what it predicts.

I am currently a Assistant Investigator at the Artificial Intelligence Research & Innovation Lab (AIRIL), Dhaka, where I design, train, and deploy ML systems and mentor junior students. I earned my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from the American International University–Bangladesh (AIUB) in 2026.

I have authored 5 peer-reviewed publications, including 2 Q1 journal articles in IEEE Access and Artificial Intelligence and Applications, presented at 3 IEEE conferences, and serve as a peer reviewer for an international conference.

🎓 I am applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027 in medical imaging, multimodal / vision-language models, and trustworthy AI. I would be glad to hear from you.

What I Bring

Explainable AI

Every published model ships with explanations — SHAP, LIME and Grad-CAM — so predictions can be checked by clinicians.

Federated & calibrated learning

Training across sites without sharing patient data, with calibration that holds up when hospitals' data differ.

LLM agents & VLMs

LangChain / LangGraph agents with RAG and vision-language models that turn model outputs into readable reports.

Research to deployment

Papers come with working real-time apps — Flask, Django, Streamlit, Docker and MLflow pipelines.
