Trustworthy AI for Healthcare

My long-term goal is to build AI systems that clinicians can trust: accurate, well-calibrated when data shifts between hospitals, and transparent about their reasoning.

Research Philosophy

A model that is 95% accurate on one hospital's data can fail silently on another's. I care about three questions for every model I build: Does it stay reliable when the data changes? Can a clinician see why it decided? Can it actually be used? That is why my projects pair the model with calibration checks, explanations, and a working deployment.
